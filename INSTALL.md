# Putting Dream House on the iPad — the 2-minute dad card

## Step 0 — get the link
Tell Claude: **"publish the game."** You'll get back ONE link (it goes live briefly, hidden from
Google, no names anywhere in it). Have the iPad in hand first.

## Install (on the iPad, on wifi) — MUST-DO steps
1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with the up-arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Tap the new **Dream House** icon on the home screen and wait for the green badge:
   **"Offline Ready ✓ v3.1"** — it must appear INSIDE the app you opened from the icon
   (not just in Safari). That's the signal everything is downloaded.
4. **Airplane-Mode test:** turn ON Airplane Mode → fully close the app → tap the icon again.
   It must open and play with no internet.
5. **Memory test (still in Airplane Mode):** type a name, pick a pet, take one photo 📸,
   place one sticker in the Bedroom → close the app fully → reopen. Name, pet, photo, and
   sticker must still be there.
6. Tell Claude: **"it works — hide it."** The link goes private; the icon keeps working offline.

Do steps 3–5 BEFORE handing the iPad to her.

## Nice-to-know
- **Her game starts fresh on install.** The old copy in the Files app used separate storage —
  nothing carries over. Updates AFTER this install keep her world.
- **If she's offline and it won't open** before the install finished: connect wifi, open it once.
- **Updating later:** I publish the new version briefly → open the app once on wifi (twice if
  needed) → look for the new version badge → tell me to hide it again.
- The old `~/Downloads/dreamhouse.html` and the iCloud `DreamHouse.html` are spare copies, not
  the app. After the icon is installed, the icon is the only thing she needs.

## For grown-ups: how to reset the game (erases EVERYTHING)
The reset is hidden from kids on purpose:
1. Press and HOLD the tiny **version** mark (says v3.1) in the top-right corner for **2 seconds**.
2. In the "Grown-ups only" screen, HOLD the **star** for **3 seconds** (keep your finger still).
3. Tap the small **"YES, erase"** button. ("Go back to playing" exits without erasing.)
